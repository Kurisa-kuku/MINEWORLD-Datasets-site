# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in MineWorld Datasets to be a respectful experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience level or research affiliation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be precise and kind. Review data and claims, not people.
- Credit recordings, builds, maps, mods and ideas.
- Respect licenses, consent and privacy.
- Assume good faith, but verify factual claims.
- Give newcomers room to ask questions.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, threats, discriminatory language or doxxing.
- Publishing personal data, private chat or access tokens.
- Deliberately misrepresenting a dataset's rights, privacy, scale or quality.
- License laundering, plagiarism or fabricated verification.
- Sustained disruption of issue threads or pull requests.

## Scope

This code applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, Giscus comments and any project space.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report serious concerns privately to the maintainers through GitHub. For security or privacy incidents, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close threads, or restrict participation. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented and avoid exposing reporters. Maintainers who are involved in an incident should recuse themselves.

This document is inspired by the Contributor Covenant, adapted for a small research-data community.
